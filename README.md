# Global Sports Footwear Market Analytics
### Strategic Pricing, Customer Segmentation & Market Optimization Framework

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a global sports footwear market dataset to identify revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, pricing opportunities, and strategic growth areas.

The analysis covers:

- Brand performance
- Discount effectiveness
- Customer segmentation
- Geographic markets
- Product portfolio optimization
- Revenue performance
- Premium positioning opportunities

The objective was to transform raw market data into actionable business recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Sports footwear brands frequently depend on discounting strategies to increase volume.

However, excessive promotions can:

- Reduce pricing power
- Erode margins
- Damage premium perception
- Increase dependency on discounts
- Lower Average Order Value (AOV)

The challenge was:

> Determine whether discount-driven growth is sustainable and identify better strategies for long-term profitability.

## Key Insights

### Insight 1 — Discount Paradox

The 0–10% discount band generated the strongest performance.

Deep discounts:

- Reduced AOV
- Failed to significantly increase volume
- Increased promotion dependency

---

### Insight 2 — Premium Customer Opportunity

Low-income women generated unexpectedly high Average Order Value.

This created an opportunity for premium product positioning.

---

### Insight 3 — Geographic Value Creation

India produced highest transaction value.

USA showed strongest premium market potential.

UAE led overall market volume.

---

### Insight 4 — Category Performance

Training category:

- Highest AOV
- Lower discount dependency
- Better profitability profile

Gym category:

- High discount reliance
- Margin pressure

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

Cap discounts at 10%.

Expected impact:

- Protect pricing power
- Maintain margins
- Preserve AOV

---

### Recommendation 2

Focus premium Training products toward high-value customer groups.

Expected impact:

- Better positioning
- Higher profitability

---

### Recommendation 3

Replace discount dependency among affluent customers with:

- Loyalty programs
- VIP access
- Early launches
- Exclusive drops

Expected impact:

Improve premium retention.
